Executive summary

The synthesis in one paragraph

ManyChat's ecommerce offering is a multi-channel (Instagram, Messenger, WhatsApp, SMS/email) chat-marketing platform whose AI answers customer questions from a per-account Knowledge Base grounded on freeform text and public links only — it does not ingest files, PDFs, or a structured product catalog, and its "Intention Recognition" is AI intent-detection layered over flow triggers rather than deep RAG over documents.

Onboarding runs through Meta's official OAuth path: businesses need an Instagram Professional (Business/Creator) account, Admin/Full-Control of the linked Facebook Page, and must toggle on "Allow Access to Messages," after which all automation is bounded by Meta's platform rules — a 24-hour messaging window from the user's last interaction, extendable to 7 days only via the support-only HUMAN_AGENT tag.

ManyChat's principal weakness is pricing: a March 2, 2026 restructure moved to contact-count tiers (Free 25, Essential $14/250, Pro $29/2,500, Business $69/7,500, Advanced $139/25,000) with continuous per-contact overages ($0.10 → $0.05 → $0.025 → $0.004), so cost scales directly with audience size — the widely-reported "success tax." For Skelo this maps to concrete differentiators: the same compliant direct-Graph-API/OAuth architecture with no BSP, plus per-org RAG grounded in the org's real catalog, CRM-native lead ownership, cross-channel voice + WhatsApp, and flat pricing instead of per-contact lock-in.

Killed claims

Why killed Not reliably documented anywhere primary; all three verifiers refuted it. Do not design throttling to this number — the real per-app/per-user ceiling is an open question to confirm against live Graph API docs.

System architecture

The essential flows, explained

Five flows carry the whole system — pick one to walk it step by step. No jargon: this is how an org gets connected, how a message becomes a lead, and how the AI and cross-channel pieces fit together on the machinery Skelo already runs.

Onboarding — connecting an org, once

How a business owner links their Instagram to Skelo. All the hard parts are ours and one-time; for the owner it's click → approve → confirm.

1
Owner clicks “Connect Instagram”
In Settings. We first check it's a Professional account (Business/Creator) and guide the free conversion if it isn't.
2
Instagram consent (OAuth)
A popup on instagram.com; the owner approves reading & sending messages and managing comments. We never see their password.
3
Token exchange
Instagram issues a 1-hour token; we immediately swap it for a 60-day one, stored encrypted and scoped to the org. This step needs no Meta approval — approval only governs who is allowed to connect.
4
Subscribe the webhook
We tell Instagram to send this account's DMs & comments to Skelo's single listener.
5
Sync the catalog
We index the org's products, FAQs & policies so the AI can answer from them — the per-org RAG knowledge base.
6
Live & self-healing
A background job refreshes the token before it expires; if the owner ever disconnects, Settings shows “reconnect needed.”
Note In development mode this works for a handful of allow-listed accounts — enough to build and run a private beta. App Review + Business Verification flips it open to the public. The token step (3) is unrelated to approval.

Inbound message → lead

The core loop that runs on every DM, comment or story reply. It mirrors, step for step, how our voice calls are already ingested.

1
Instagram notifies our webhook
One URL receives events for every connected org.
2
Verify it's really Instagram
A signature check on every event; forged payloads are dropped before anything happens.
3
Identify the org
From the trusted account ID in the payload — never from anything the message itself claims.
4
Ignore duplicates
Instagram sometimes redelivers; idempotency keys ensure it never creates two leads or two replies.
5
Find or create the lead
Matched on Instagram identity, filed under the right organisation_id — same logic our calls use.
6
Understand & answer
AI reads intent and drafts a reply grounded in the org's catalog (RAG); rules auto-send or hand to a human.
7
Save everything
Message + reply attached to the lead, visible beside its calls & WhatsApp history.
Why Steps 2–4 are the security & reliability backbone — the exact posture we already run on our call webhooks.

Comment → DM funnel

Turn a public commenter into a private conversation — the highest-intent moment on Instagram.

1
Someone comments
e.g. “price?” on a post or reel.
2
Public reply
A short, friendly nudge: “Just DM'd you the details 💬”.
3
Private DM
The real answer — price, links — pulled from the catalog, sent within the 24-hour window.
4
Lead created & tagged
The commenter becomes a Hot lead with the whole thread attached.
Policy This is Meta's blessed pattern — public comment automation is policy-sensitive, so moving to a DM is the safe funnel. We design toward it, not against it.

Follow-ups & sequences

Timed outreach — welcomes, nudges, qualifying questions — without a new scheduling system and without breaking Instagram's rules.

1
The every-minute scheduler wakes
The same one already dispatching our calls & WhatsApp campaigns.
2
Pick what's due
Welcome DMs, gentle nudges when there's no reply, qualifying questions.
3
24-hour guard
Before each send, confirm we're still inside Instagram's window; if not, skip or reroute — never send something that would be blocked.
4
Send & log
Deliver on Instagram and record it on the lead.
Reuse No new infrastructure — Instagram becomes a 5th outreach type on the scheduler that already handles four.

Cross-channel recovery

When Instagram's rules won't let us reach someone, use another door — on the same lead. This is the flow a single-channel tool structurally can't run.

1
Trigger
The 24-hour window has closed, or the lead is hot enough to warrant a call.
2
Choose a channel
WhatsApp (a template send via our BSP) or a voice-agent callback.
3
Reach out
On the same lead, continuing the same conversation — not a cold restart.
4
Back to one record
The reply loops into the lead's unified timeline, beside its Instagram, WhatsApp and call history.
Edge This is precisely where ManyChat's per-channel model leaves money on the table — abandoned-cart re-engagement outside the 24h window can't be done on IG DM alone, but our WhatsApp + voice fallback picks it up.
Foundation · under every flow
Your CRM database — every message, lead and conversion, scoped to your organisation. No middleman holds your data; nothing is metered per contact.
